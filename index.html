About Us

Welcome to Healing Hands Genuine Massage Center, a professional massage therapy and holistic wellness center based in Kamothe, Mumbai. We are dedicated to helping you relieve stress, ease muscle tension, and restore balance in your body and mind.

Our team consists of certified massage therapists who have trained in various techniques such as Swedish Massage, Deep tissue, sports massage, Fascial release, and therapeutic holistic massage. We also integrate gentle stretching and relaxation practices to support long‑term recovery and better posture.


Why choose us?

  • Personalized sessions: Every treatment is tailored to your body, pain areas, and lifestyle (office work, cycling, sports, etc.).
  • Safe, clean space: We maintain high hygiene standards, including sanitized equipment and fresh linens for each session.
  • Holistic approach: Along with massage, we offer simple guidance on posture, stretching, and self‑care so you feel better between visits.

Whether you are recovering from injury, managing chronic pain, or simply want to unwind after a long day, our mission is to provide gentle, professional, and effective care that supports your overall well‑being.
